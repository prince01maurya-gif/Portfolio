I am a specialized SQL Developer & Database Developer with a laser focus on high-throughput database design, stored procedure logic, and deep performance tuning. While frontend interfaces evolve rapidly, the database remains the immutable single source of truth for business data.

My daily work revolves around SQL Server, PostgreSQL, and MySQL—architecting 3NF normalized schemas, engineering mission-critical stored procedures with Table-Valued Parameters, and eliminating slow queries before they hit production.

Whether unraveling a complex deadlock graph, turning a 45,000-read table scan into a targeted index seek, or building double-entry accounting ledgers with strict ACID guarantees, I write SQL that scales gracefully under intense transactional load.
